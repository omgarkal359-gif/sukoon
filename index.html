About Sukoon

We are a dedicated group of open source contributors who created Sukoon to provide a gentle, one-step solution to help you manage stress and restore peace of mind.

Our mission is simple: empower everyone to live a calmer, stress-free life through accessible mental health therapies and self-care resources.
